Kana Reading Drills

読Kana Reading Drills

Kana only, no kanji. Words are spaced to help you start. Turn spaces off once you can find word boundaries yourself, the way real Japanese is written.

As particles, は is read "wa", を is "o", and へ is "e". Sentences use polite です/ます forms, which are safe with anyone, unless a note says they're casual.

Every word was checked against the JMdict Japanese–English dictionary and a Japanese grammar parser.
